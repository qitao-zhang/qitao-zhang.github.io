<p class="academic-lead">Sustainable subsurface energy infrastructure</p>

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Center for Subsurface Energy and Environment, within the Hildebrand Department of Petroleum and Geosystems Engineering at The University of Texas at Austin. I received my Ph.D. in Energy and Mineral Engineering, with a minor in Computational Science, from The Pennsylvania State University in 2024, where I subsequently served as a Postdoctoral Scholar.

My research focuses on geothermal energy, geologic carbon storage, and long-duration underground energy storage. I study how fluid flow, heat transport, and rock deformation shape the performance and reliability of these systems, with the goal of advancing resilient, low-carbon energy infrastructure.

[Research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) · [Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) · [Download CV (PDF)]({{ '/files/Qitao_Zhang_CV.pdf' | relative_url }})

## Research directions

- **Geothermal energy:** Understanding thermal short-circuiting and regulating fracture flow to improve heat recovery and sustain reservoir performance.
- **Infrastructure reuse and energy storage:** Repurposing abandoned wells and mines for geothermal heat, underground energy storage, and low-carbon cooling.
- **Geologic carbon storage:** Evaluating CO₂ plume evolution, containment, and geomechanical risk in heterogeneous subsurface systems.
